What we collect, and why.

When you request design-partner access we store the email address you submit, and — only if you choose to provide them — your node count, deployment type, and a short note. We use these solely to contact you about the OSTIR design-partner program.

We do not sell or share this information with third parties, and we do not use third-party trackers or advertising cookies on this site. To have your entry removed, email hello@ostir.ai.
